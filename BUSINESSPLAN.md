# Executive Summary

MyChef is an innovative AI-driven meal planning application that leverages the Google Gemini AI API to revolutionize the way busy families plan and prepare meals.

The app offers highly personalized recipe suggestions and image generation, taking into account:

- Diet preferences
- Allergies
- Disliked ingredients
- Seasonal ingredients
- Family size
- Meal type
- Preparation time
- Budget

Users can further customize their experience by setting the number of weekly meals, reviewing and modifying preferences, and updating seasonal options.

MyChef simplifies meal planning through:

- Personalized meal plans
- Smart shopping lists
- Recipe regeneration
- Offline shopping-list access
- Image-to-recipe generation

The goal is to save time, improve cooking experiences, promote healthy eating habits, and reduce food waste.

---

# Mission Statement

To simplify and enhance weekly meal planning for busy families by providing personalized, seasonal recipes tailored to diverse tastes, budget constraints, and preparation times.

We aim to make cooking and dining enjoyable through an AI-powered experience that includes smart shopping lists while promoting healthier eating habits and reducing food waste.

# Products and Services

MyChef Meal Planner offers the following key features:

## AI-Powered Recipe Generation

Utilizing the Google Gemini AI API, the app generates unique recipes based on user preferences, dietary needs, and available ingredients.

## Customized Meal Plans

Weekly meal plans tailored to each family's needs and schedules.

## Automated Grocery Lists

Automatically generated grocery lists based on selected meal plans.

## Recipe Image Generation

AI-generated images corresponding to each recipe for visual inspiration and guidance.

## Eating Preferences

Users can specify:

- Dietary restrictions
- Allergens
- Disliked ingredients
- Seasonal preferences
- Family size
- Desired preparation time
- Budget

## Image-to-Recipe Generation

Users can upload:

- Dish images
- Ingredient photos

The AI generates corresponding recipes, helping users recreate meals or build meal plans from available ingredients.

## Custom Recipes Management

Users can:

- Save favorite recipes
- Upload personal recipes
- Maintain a personal recipe library
- Import recipes from URLs through web scraping
